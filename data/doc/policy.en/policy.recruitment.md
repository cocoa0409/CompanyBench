# Molly Digital Recruitment Policy

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 Purpose

This policy is established to standardize the recruitment management processes of Molly Digital (hereinafter referred to as "the Company"), ensuring that talent acquisition is conducted in a scientific, fair, and efficient manner.

### Article 2 Scope of Application

This policy applies to recruitment activities for all formal positions across the Company's departments, including job posting, resume evaluation, interviewing, hiring, and compensation determination.

### Article 3 Fundamental Principles

(1) Openness and transparency: All open positions must be published on the Company's official careers page to ensure public access to information.

(2) Merit-based hiring: Guided by position requirements, the Company adheres to the principle of matching competency to position and selects the best-qualified candidates.

(3) Legal compliance: The entire recruitment process must comply with applicable laws and regulations as well as the Company's cultural values.

## Chapter 2 Job Posting

### Article 4 Posting Prerequisites

A hiring department may initiate the recruitment process only when approved headcount (HC) is available. Recruitment requests are initiated by the hiring department's manager or executive leadership.

### Article 5 Job Description (JD) Requirements

Job descriptions must include the following mandatory elements:

(1) Job title;

(2) Department;

(3) Work location (base location options);

(4) Job responsibilities;

(5) Qualifications;

(6) Preferred qualifications (bonus criteria);

(7) Compensation range: Calculated as monthly salary × 16 (comprising 12 months of base salary plus 4 months of year-end bonus);

(8) Contact information: The contact details of the department's designated HRBP shall be uniformly provided.

### Article 6 Posting Management

(1) Job descriptions are prepared by the hiring department's manager and, upon review, published on the Company's official careers page.

(2) Job description content must align with the Company's cultural values, and wording should be professional, accurate, and standardized.

## Chapter 3 Resume Submission and Evaluation

### Article 7 Submission Requirements

When submitting a resume for a formal position at the Company, candidates must associate their application with one and only one job description (JD). Simultaneous applications to multiple positions are not permitted.

### Article 8 Resume Evaluation

Upon receipt of a candidate's resume, the hiring department's manager evaluates the resume and makes one of the following decisions:

(1) The candidate passes evaluation and proceeds to the interview process;

(2) The candidate does not pass evaluation; the recruitment process is terminated and the candidate is notified accordingly.

## Chapter 4 Interview Process

### Article 9 Interview Rounds

The interview process consists of four sequential rounds:

| Round | Name | Interviewer |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Round 1 | Hiring Manager Interview | Hiring department manager |
| Round 2 | Leadership Interview | Direct supervisor of the hiring department manager |
| Round 3 | HRBP Interview | Designated HRBP for the department |
| Round 4 | Compensation Discussion | Head of the Human Resources department |

### Article 10 Interview Scoring Criteria

Interviewers must evaluate candidates according to the following scoring scale:

| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 3.00 | Not Recommended |
| 3.25 | Average |
| 3.50 | Recommended |
| 3.75 | Highly Recommended |
| 4.00 | Strongly Needed |

### Article 11 Scoring Deadline

Interviewers must complete their scoring within 2 business days after the interview. Failure to do so is treated as a process exception and will be followed up by the HRBP.

### Article 12 Fast-Track Mechanism

If the average score of Round 1 and Round 2 exceeds 3.75 (i.e., both interviewers rate the candidate as "Highly Recommended" or above), Round 3 (HRBP Interview) may be bypassed to prevent the loss of outstanding talent, and the candidate proceeds directly to Round 4 (Compensation Discussion).

### Article 13 Hiring Criteria

(1) Candidates whose average score across three interview rounds (or two rounds under the fast-track mechanism per Article 12) is greater than or equal to 3.50 shall be offered employment and proceed to the compensation discussion.

(2) Candidates whose average score does not reach 3.50 shall not be offered employment, and the recruitment process is terminated.

## Chapter 5 Compensation Determination

### Article 14 Compensation Negotiation Ceiling

During the compensation discussion, the salary offered to the candidate (calculated as monthly salary × 16, including 4 months of year-end bonus) shall not exceed 120% of the upper limit of the compensation range stated in the job description.

## Chapter 6 Supplementary Provisions

### Article 15 Right of Interpretation

This policy shall be interpreted and revised by the Company's Human Resources department.

### Article 16 Effective Date

This policy shall take effect on January 27, 2022.
